Download Twitter videos with the post text

ClipTweet turns a public X (Twitter) post into an MP4 with its text above the video. It is a free Twitter video downloader for making a clip that keeps the post's context, rather than a download of the unchanged source video. No account or installation is required.

How to download an X video as an MP4

  1. Copy the link to a public X or Twitter post containing a video.
  2. Paste it above and select Generate Clip.
  3. Preview your clip and select Download MP4.

ClipTweet processes the video in your browser. Keep this tab open until the clip is ready.

Frequently asked questions

Are downloads free?

Yes. ClipTweet provides a complete generation flow free of charge. Display advertising may help fund operation; allowing ads is optional, and ad blockers do not prevent generating or downloading. No feature is unlocked by interacting with ads.

Which posts work?

Public posts with a playable video and duration metadata. Private, protected or deleted posts cannot be downloaded. Some animated GIF posts lack duration metadata and are not supported.

What are the limits?

Videos must be no longer than 10 minutes; the generated MP4 must fit within 20 MB. Some devices or codecs are unsupported. Use an up-to-date browser with WebCodecs support.

Does ClipTweet store my videos?

No. Your browser downloads media directly from X's video CDN and creates the output locally. Our resolver only retrieves post metadata.

How does ClipTweet stay free?

ClipTweet may show independent display advertising around the tool. Allowing ads is optional and helps support maintenance; it is never required to generate or download a clip.

Can I use an ad blocker?

Yes. Ad blockers and Brave Shields do not affect generation or downloads. You can dismiss the optional notice.
