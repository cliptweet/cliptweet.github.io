About ClipTweet

ClipTweet is an independent web tool for turning supported public X posts with video or animated GIFs into MP4 clips. It keeps the post's text with the media, so the clip carries its context.

Standard places text above the video and offers optional personalization and ClipTweet branding. Reel places text over the video. The aim is a simple flow: paste a link, choose a layout, create a clip.

How ClipTweet works

  1. Paste a supported X post link and choose Standard or Reel.
  2. Select Generate Clip. ClipTweet retrieves the post text and media information needed for your clip.
  3. Your browser creates the MP4. Preview it, then download.

The final video is created on your own device, directly in your browser. Keep this tab open until it is ready.

The post link goes to ClipTweet's API for lookup; your browser then downloads the video from X. See Privacy for details about these connections.

Frequently asked questions

Are downloads free?

Yes. ClipTweet provides a complete generation flow free of charge. Display advertising may help fund operation; allowing ads is optional, and ad blockers do not prevent generating or downloading. No feature is unlocked by interacting with ads.

Which posts work?

Public posts with a playable video or animated GIF. Private, protected or deleted posts cannot be downloaded. GIF timing is read from its normalized video track when X omits duration metadata.

What are the limits?

Videos must be no longer than 10 minutes and smaller than 1.2 GB. Choose a maximum output file size; it is a ceiling, not a target. Some devices or codecs are unsupported. Use an up-to-date browser with WebCodecs support.

Does ClipTweet store my videos?

No. Your browser downloads media directly from X's video CDN and creates the output locally. Our resolver only retrieves post metadata.

How does ClipTweet stay free?

ClipTweet may show independent display advertising around the tool. Allowing ads is optional and helps support maintenance; it is never required to generate or download a clip.

Can I use an ad blocker?

Yes. Ad blockers and Brave Shields do not affect generation or downloads. You can dismiss the optional notice.

Privacy

ClipTweet sends the post link to its Cloudflare Worker to retrieve public metadata from X. Your browser contacts X's video CDN and jsDelivr for the video-processing library. These providers receive connection information such as your IP address. Generated videos remain on your device; ClipTweet does not upload or store them.

Cloudflare processes resolver requests and may retain operational logs. Generated videos remain on your device.
